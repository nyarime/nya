## How to read

- **multi=false**: single on-disk chunk (legacy path); workers ignored on create/extract.
- **multi=true**: format v1.3 split at 4 MiB raw boundaries.
- **createW / extractW**: `-workers` on `nya create` / `nya extract` (Reader.SetWorkers).
- **repair**: one middle chunk corrupted (512 B zeroed); per-chunk FEC rebuild.
- **heapΔ create**: heap in-use delta after archive creation (approximate; GC between samples).
