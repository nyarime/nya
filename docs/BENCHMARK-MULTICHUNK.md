## How to read

- **multi=false**: single on-disk chunk (legacy path); workers ignored.
- **multi=true**: format v1.3 split at 4 MiB raw boundaries; `-workers` parallelizes chunk compression only.
- **extract**: sequential today (no reader-side parallel decompress yet).
- **repair**: one middle chunk corrupted (512 B zeroed); per-chunk FEC rebuild.
- **heapΔ create**: heap in-use delta after archive creation (approximate; GC between samples).

## Findings

Measured on Cloud Agent VM (Aug 2026), structured text payload, level 7, `-fec 10`:

1. **Compress speedup**: multi-chunk + `-workers 4/8` yields **~3.7×** faster create vs single-chunk at 32 MiB; **~3.4–3.8×** at 100 MiB (3.8s → 1.0s with 8 workers).
2. **Ratio unchanged**: archive size within 0.03% of single-chunk baseline (same codec, independent chunk boundaries).
3. **Extract flat**: reader decompresses chunks sequentially — no material speedup yet (~850–915 ms at 100 MiB regardless of workers).
4. **Per-chunk FEC repair**: corrupting one middle chunk repairs in **~4–8 ms**; verify stays sub-millisecond.
5. **Memory**: heap after extract ~1.2 MiB (32 MiB file) / ~2.3 MiB (100 MiB file); parallel create does not spike heap materially on this VM.

**Next steps**: reader-side parallel decompress for multi-chunk entries; re-run on bare metal with `-workers` matching CPU count.
